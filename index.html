Welcome to Sasnaka Ape Aurudu 2026

Welcome to Sasnaka Ape Aurudu 2026, a vibrant celebration of Sri Lankan New Year traditions, unity, and cultural pride. This platform is designed to manage and showcase the exciting Aurudu Kumara and Aurudu Kumariya competitions for 2026.

Through this event, we aim to bring together participants from across the island to celebrate the spirit of Avurudu with traditional games, cultural performances, and joyful gatherings. Contestants will have the opportunity to express their talents, personality, and cultural values while competing for these prestigious titles.

Join us in celebrating tradition, building connections, and creating unforgettable memories as part of Sasnaka Ape Aurudu 2026!
